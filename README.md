## Description

**MARCADORES-CLI** is a **CLI** program inteded to get sports results and scores through your preferred terminal. All the information comes from: **www.flashscore.com**, the greatest scoreboard, news and sport-related webpage!

Football, tennis, basketball, any sport within the reach of your terminal
and some keystrokes!
